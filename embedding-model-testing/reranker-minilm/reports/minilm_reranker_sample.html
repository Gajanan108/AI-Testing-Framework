Finding validation criteria

  • Demonstrate a repeatable ranking inversion or material ranking degradation.
  • Show that the perturbation can be realised within a credible attacker capability and application boundary.
  • Measure downstream impact using retrieval metrics such as MRR, NDCG@k, and Recall@k, plus relevant RAG/application outcomes.
  • Check semantic preservation, perturbation constraints, query/candidate diversity, and reproducibility across seeds.
  • Assign true/false finding status only after evidence review. Do not label a latent-space class flip as a confirmed production vulnerability.

6. Threat-model limitations

ART operates on continuous internal word embeddings in this experiment. A perturbed embedding is not automatically convertible into a valid natural-language text edit. Therefore, latent-space ranking changes alone do not establish that an attacker can reproduce the effect through a public text-input endpoint.
  • Does not directly test token-level or natural-language adversarial edits.
  • Does not test the retriever, vector database, corpus poisoning, access control, or downstream RAG orchestration.
  • Single-pair smoke tests are insufficient for representative robustness conclusions.
  • OWASP mapping must follow demonstrated application impact, not the name of an attack algorithm.
