How to interpret these reports

Execution status and finding status are separate. A failed attack is not a successful test; an observed score or class change is not automatically a vulnerability. Confirmed findings require reproducible evidence of security-relevant impact under a stated threat model.

For the latest notebook status, attack applicability, and limitations, see the model workstream READMEs in this repository.
